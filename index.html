Common Questions

Before you reach out.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We visit the property, take measurements, and provide a written, itemized estimate at no cost and no obligation.

How long does a custom furniture piece take?

Most single pieces, like a table or bookshelf, take two to four weeks depending on material availability and finish. We confirm a timeline before starting.

Do you handle permits for larger renovations?

For projects that require township permits, we prepare the paperwork and coordinate inspections as part of the project timeline.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Piscataway and regularly work throughout Middlesex and Somerset counties — see the full service area below.

Do you offer a warranty on your work?

Yes. Workmanship is covered after completion, and we'll come back to address any defect in materials or installation.
